BookBubble Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 7, 2026

BookBubble ("the app") is a personal reading-library app. This policy explains what data the app handles and how.

Summary

BookBubble does not have user accounts, does not require sign-in, and does not send your library, notes, or photos to any server operated by us. All of your book data, notes, and images are stored locally on your device.

Data stored on your device

The following is saved only on your own device, using local app storage:

  • Your saved books and their details (title, author, reading status, progress, etc.)
  • Notes you write, including any tags and locations you add
  • Photos you attach to notes (plain pictures and scanned quote images), stored in the app's private local storage
  • Your app preferences (e.g. date format, progress bar setting)

This data is not transmitted to us or to any third party. It stays on your device unless you choose to export it (see below).

Camera and photo library access

BookBubble requests camera and photo library permissions so you can attach pictures to notes and scan quotes from a book page. Photos you choose are saved to the app's local storage on your device and are not uploaded anywhere.

On-device text recognition (OCR)

When you scan a quote, BookBubble uses on-device text recognition (Google ML Kit) to read text from the image you select. This processing happens entirely on your device. No image or recognized text is sent to any external server for this purpose.

Book search (Open Library)

When you search for a book by title, author, or ISBN, BookBubble sends your search terms to the Open Library API (openlibrary.org), an external, third-party service, to retrieve book information and cover images. No account information, notes, or personal data is sent as part of these requests — only the search text you enter. Please refer to Open Library's own policies for how they handle these requests.

Data export and deletion

You can export a backup of your books and notes as a JSON file from within the app (Settings), and you can clear all local app data from the same screen at any time. Since BookBubble does not store your data on any server, deleting the app or clearing its data removes your information entirely — we do not retain a copy.

Data we do not collect

BookBubble does not collect analytics, does not use advertising identifiers, does not require any account or sign-in, and does not share data with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

Children's privacy

BookBubble does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children, since no personal information is collected or transmitted by the app at all.

Changes to this policy

If BookBubble's data practices change in the future (for example, if account-based features are introduced), this policy will be updated accordingly, and the "Last updated" date above will reflect that change.
